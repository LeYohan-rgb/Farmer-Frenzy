# Farmer Frenzy

<img width="350" height="204" alt="farmer_frenzy_game_logo" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9a1533d7-5973-44a1-9f92-5a971cf5fab7" />

Welcome to Farmer Frenzy: a battle game where two farmers fight by collecting beans from the ground to harvest special fruits to aid them in battle, each one with a different power! The last farmer standing wins.
You can play it here: https://yohan-xx.itch.io/farmer-frenzy <br>
(<i>NOTE</i>: I submitted this project to Macondo, a Hackclub event, where it got starfruited! See it here: https://macondo.hackclub.com/projects/5862)

<strong>INSPIRATION</strong> <br>
One of my first games was a spaceship battle game on Godot where each player had unique abilities (blackholes, redirection bullets, etc). I quickly moved on to other projects, but the idea to make an ability-based game stayed on my mind. Now, that was my inspiration for the gameplay mechanic. Farmer Frenzy was originally going to be a typical farming game, clearly inspired by the fruits from Macondo. I didn't find the gameplay entertaining, so I settled on two contrasting themes: a fighting and a 'farming' game.

# Gameplay

<img width="576" height="326" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/0eb51c5b-adf4-48f0-a178-25220aa5d0ad" />

Farmer Frenzy has two main game-modes: Farmer Fight (two-player) and Harvest Rush (singleplayer). Farmer Fight is the main one, and you need to purchase fruits on the Fruitpedia to use them in battle. The game currency is the pesos, and you obtain them by collecting fruits in the Harvest Rush mode.

<img width="276" height="146" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e5f74a7c-dc85-478b-92ce-6118ee81c196" />

<strong>SETTINGS</strong> <br>
You are able to change the farmer's names used on the Farmer Fight mode. By default, their names are 'Emiliano' and 'Jerónimo' (typical Colombian names). Additionaly, you can keep track of how many wins each farmer has next to their names.

<img width="473" height="231" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/3bf642de-00f8-4971-91dd-5f6d17dea684" />

<strong>FRUITPEDIA</strong> <br>

The Fruitpedia serves as the 'encyclopedia' and shop of the game. Here, you can view a detailed explanation of each fruit used on the Farmer Fight mode, as well as their <strong>cooldown</strong> and <strong>bean price</strong>. <i>(See Farmer Fight Gameplay)</i> Moreover, you are able to see how much each fruit costs in pesos and buy them. By default, the player has the Mango, Watermelon, and Papaya.

<strong>FARMER FIGHT</strong> <br>

<img width="574" height="327" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/43326b65-401c-441a-8bb0-096692ef792e" />

Before each battle, farmers are free to choose which purchased fruits to use and arrange them in their slots as they like. After selecting their fruits, a battle can commence.

<img width="577" height="332" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2123945d-2ae3-4a01-9102-799ba436cd12" />

Each farmer counts with their three fruit slots, 10 kernels, and a shield. The goal is to defeat the other farmer by lowering their health down to zero. Each farmer has their own side of the field, and they cannot pass to the other side as the river serves as the main boundary; the only way to defeat the opposing farmer is to use kernels and fruits.

<i>KERNELS:</i>
Kernels are the primary source of damage of each farmer, and they act as normal 'bullets'. They each count with 10 starting kernels, and after using them all they must wait 3 seconds to charge them again.

<i>SHIELD:</i>
The shield is the primary defensive option of each farmer. It counts with a fifth of the farmer's health, and they serve to block upcoming projectiles. While using it, players cannot use kernels nor their fruits and viceversa.

<i>BEANS:</i>
As the battle progresses, more and more beans will come out of the ground. Farmers need to collect them so that they can be used to harvest the farmer's fruits--their strongest weapons. 

<i>FRUIT SLOTS:</i>
Each fruit has two main stats: bean cost and cooldown. Each fruit costs a certain amount of beans to use. After harvesting one, farmers must wait until the fruit's cooldown ends before harvesting it again.

# Controls

<i>FIRST FARMER</i>

<ul>
    <li>MOVEMENT: WASD</li>
    <li>SHOOT KERNEL: E</li>
    <li>SHIELD: Q</li>
    <li>FRUIT ABILITIES: '1', '2', and '3'</li>
</ul>

<i>SECOND FARMER</i>

<ul>
    <li>MOVEMENT: Arrow Keys</li>
    <li>SHOOT KERNEL: Right Ctrl</li>
    <li>SHIELD: Right Shift</li>
    <li>FRUIT ABILITIES: 'Comma (,)', 'Period (.)', and 'Slash (/)'</li>
</ul>
